Why Mochimono

Stop organizing storage.
Organize what you see.

Your archive should remember the files underneath everything—not force you to maintain the perfect folder tree forever.

◎

One copy, every place

Exact duplicates are stored once, even if they came from different phones, drives, folders, or old backups.

⌂

Cloud where you need it

Your main library stays accessible from anywhere while inexpensive local hard drives act as disaster-recovery copies.

✓

Know what is protected

Mochimono keeps an inventory of your backup drives so you can see what is backed up, where it lives, and what still needs a copy.

How it works

Dump first. Sort later.

The boring storage layer stays simple; Mochimono adds the useful memory on top.

Drop it in

Point the local Agent at a folder, old computer backup, or an entire drive.

Hash & deduplicate

Files are identified by their contents, so identical bytes never need another full copy.

Browse anywhere

Your cloud library becomes the convenient home for finding and opening your files.

Back it up

Connect a backup drive and Mochimono copies only the objects that drive is missing.

The idea

Your files should be easier to keep than to lose.

Mochimono is being built as a small, practical personal archive—not another folder-sync app. It remembers content identity, where things came from, intentional deletions, and which storage locations protect each file.
